Quick Drop Zone

Use this area for fast temporary storage when you need to save screenshots, snippets, or references immediately during an active development session.

Staging Before Sorting

Treat this section like a staging layer before final organization. Once an asset clearly belongs somewhere else, move it into the proper workspace section for long-term clarity.

Recommended use: session overflow, temporary notes, intermediate files, working drafts.

Suggested Temp Workflow

  • Drop files here quickly when you are in the middle of active work.
  • Keep names simple and descriptive so they are easy to find later.
  • Review this section regularly and move important assets into their proper categories.
  • Delete stale or unused temporary files so this area stays useful.

Purpose of This Section

The Temp workspace reduces friction during active development. It gives the Satoshium build process a practical landing zone for unfinished or unsorted materials without disrupting the structure of the rest of the workspace.
