# 🧪 Satoshium Labs

Satoshium Labs contains interactive tools, experimental interfaces,
simulations, and prototype systems used to explore decentralized intelligence,
AI governance, trust infrastructure, and long-horizon coordination models.

Labs provides a safe environment for structured experimentation before systems
migrate into permanent platform layers.

---

# 🧭 Purpose

Satoshium Labs allows concepts to move from architecture into observable
systems.

This layer supports:

- prototype intelligence interfaces
- governance simulations
- trust-verification workflows
- infrastructure stress models
- glossary and knowledge engines
- coordination experiments

Labs is where platform ideas become interactive.
