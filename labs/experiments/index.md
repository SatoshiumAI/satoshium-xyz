# 🧪 Satoshium Labs — Experimental Modules

This directory documents experimental modules currently available within
the Satoshium Labs environment.

These modules explore decentralized truth verification, sovereign infrastructure
signals, scenario intelligence, and cryptographic governance models.

All systems listed here are experimental and may evolve, migrate, or be retired
as the platform develops.

The experiments layer exposes prototype systems that support exploration of
future Satoshium capabilities before integration into permanent platform layers.

Experiments help evaluate:

- long-horizon intelligence interfaces
