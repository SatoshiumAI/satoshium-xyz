# 🧪 satoshium.xyz

Experimental systems and simulation environments for the Satoshium platform.

The **satoshium.xyz repository** defines the frontier laboratory layer of the
Satoshium ecosystem. This domain supports exploration of new interfaces,
agents, simulations, and coordination models before they become permanent
components of the platform architecture.

---

# 🧭 Purpose

satoshium.xyz exists to provide a structured environment for experimentation
across decentralized intelligence infrastructure.

It supports:

- prototype agent systems
- simulation environments
- interface experiments
- coordination models
- research-stage infrastructure concepts

Experiments developed here may later migrate into:

satoshium.ai
satoshium.net
satoshium.info

after refinement and validation.

---

Within the Satoshium domain structure:
